# Classfolio roadmap

1. **Public landing page and workspace preview.** The public landing page introduces the product and school setup. The separate workspace preview shows supported class curricula without fictional student identities or scores; live score entry and reports must use saved school records.
2. **School registration and tenant foundations — implemented; live setup requires Netlify configuration.** Schools can register after email verification and receive isolated workspaces. New workspaces are seeded with 14 JSS 1/2 subjects, 17 JSS 3 subjects, and 18 SS 1–3 subjects in a school-owned catalogue. Administrators can optionally add editable Nigerian, British/Cambridge, or Turkish/MEB subject suggestions, then choose subjects independently for each class. The selected class subjects drive its score-sheet and report-preview subject lists. Packs are editable suggestions, not official grade-by-grade mappings. SS students' eventual 10–11-subject selection workflow is not yet enforced. Administrators can add subjects before enrolling students, manage staff roles, and assign teachers. Existing school catalogues are not overwritten automatically. Netlify Database stores school records; Netlify Identity authenticates staff.
3. **Persistent score-entry foundation — implemented; integration incomplete.** Server operations save and validate unpublished draft scores with tenant, membership, role, assignment, enrollment, subject, and term checks. The workspace no longer shows sample students or scores. Saved records are not yet connected to visible score entry or report generation.
4. **School-branded reporting and results review.** School setup and administrator profile settings save each school's name, address, optional logo, contact details, motto, and main/accent colours; red and sky blue are the defaults, with contrast warnings for hard-to-read choices. JSS 1/2, JSS 3, and SS 1–3 have term-specific blank report previews based on their supplied layouts: first/second-term design for the first two terms and a third-term design for the third term. SS uses its senior subject list on both term layouts. These templates do not calculate grades, averages, remarks, or rankings. Connect the saved roster and scores to the layouts, then implement approval, publication, and audit workflows. Confirm junior grade, average, remark, and ranking rules with the school before calculating them.
5. **Annual results and family access.** Implement the ranking-sheet workflow, school-approved annual aggregation and promotion rules, historical comparisons, secure parent access, and batch report downloads. Validate formulas against the workbook and approved edge cases before production use.

The supplied senior workbook's term- and subject-specific grade/remark thresholds, competition ranking (including ties and no rank for non-positive results), third-term three-term subject averages rounded to one decimal place, and thirteen-subject aggregate divided by ten are intentional. Preserve their regression tests and do not normalize the thresholds or divisor. Test and examination score limits are 40 and 60.
