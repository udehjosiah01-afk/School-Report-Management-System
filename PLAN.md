# Classfolio roadmap

1. **Public landing page and workspace preview.** The public landing page introduces the product and school setup. The separate workspace preview shows supported class curricula without fictional student identities or scores; live score entry and reports must use saved school records.
2. **School registration and tenant foundations — implemented; live setup requires Netlify configuration.** Schools can register after email verification and receive isolated workspaces. Each workspace is seeded with JSS 1–3, SS 1–3, and a school-owned subject catalogue based on the supplied curricula. Administrators select subjects independently for each class and can add subjects to support different curricula before enrolling students. They can enroll students, manage staff roles, and assign teachers. Netlify Database stores school records; Netlify Identity authenticates staff.
3. **Persistent score-entry foundation — implemented; integration incomplete.** Server operations save and validate unpublished draft scores with tenant, membership, role, assignment, enrollment, subject, and term checks. The workspace no longer shows sample students or scores. Saved records are not yet connected to visible score entry or report generation.
4. **School-branded reporting and results review.** School setup and administrator profile settings save each school's name, address, optional logo, contact details, motto, and main/accent colours; red and sky blue are the defaults, with contrast warnings for hard-to-read choices. JSS 1/2 use the supplied first/second-term layout for those terms and the term-comparison layout for third term; JSS 3 and SS templates remain pending their supplied designs. The report layout includes the subject grid, reference scale, domains, and comment fields. Connect the saved roster and scores to the layout, then implement approval, publication, and audit workflows. Confirm junior grade, average, remark, and ranking rules with the school before calculating them.
5. **Annual results and family access.** Implement the ranking-sheet workflow, school-approved annual aggregation and promotion rules, historical comparisons, secure parent access, and batch report downloads. Validate formulas against the workbook and approved edge cases before production use.

The supplied senior workbook's term- and subject-specific grade/remark thresholds, competition ranking (including ties and no rank for non-positive results), third-term three-term subject averages rounded to one decimal place, and thirteen-subject aggregate divided by ten are intentional. Preserve their regression tests and do not normalize the thresholds or divisor. Test and examination score limits are 40 and 60.
