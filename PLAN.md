# Classfolio roadmap

1. **Public landing page and workspace preview.** The public landing page introduces the product and school setup. The separate workspace preview shows supported class curricula without fictional student identities or scores; live score entry and reports must use saved school records.
2. **School registration and tenant foundations — implemented; live setup requires Netlify configuration.** Schools can register after email verification and receive isolated workspaces. New workspaces are seeded with 14 JSS 1/2 subjects, 17 JSS 3 subjects, and 18 SS 1–3 subjects in a school-owned catalogue. Administrators can optionally add editable Nigerian, British/Cambridge, or Turkish/MEB subject suggestions, then choose subjects independently for each class. The selected class subjects drive its score-sheet and report-preview subject lists. Packs are editable suggestions, not official grade-by-grade mappings. The administrator selects each learner's registered subjects; SS learners must be registered for 10 or 11 configured subjects, and the overall-average calculation uses that learner's selected-subject count. Administrators can add subjects before enrolling students, manage staff roles, and assign teachers. Existing school catalogues are not overwritten automatically. Netlify Database stores school records; Netlify Identity authenticates staff.
3. **Persistent score entry and teacher access — implemented; deployment configuration required.** Administrators and assigned teachers can load score sheets for enrolled students and save draft scores. Server operations enforce tenant, membership, role, assignment, enrollment, subject, term, and score-limit checks. Teacher access requires staff sign-in plus a subject-specific four-digit code; administrators generate missing codes when opening the workspace and can reset a code. Newly issued codes are shown once for private distribution. Failed attempts lock a code temporarily.
4. **School-branded reports, learner photos, and publication — implemented; school data and live deployment still require validation.** School setup and administrator profile settings save each school's name, address, optional logo, contact details, motto, and main/accent colours; red and sky blue are the defaults, with contrast warnings for hard-to-read choices. Logos appear as faint report watermarks. Learner passport photos can be uploaded during enrollment or updated from the roster and appear on reports. JSS 1/2, JSS 3, and SS 1–3 use their configured class subjects and term-specific layouts. Administrators can publish a term only after every registered subject score has both test and examination marks; publication locks the term's draft scores. Published reports apply the supplied workbook's term-specific grades, remarks, competition positions, class subject averages, and third-term subject averaging across class levels. The overall average divides by the learner's selected-subject count; senior enrollment is limited to 10 or 11 configured subjects. Validate calculations against representative school records before production use.
5. **Parent access and remaining results workflows — partially implemented.** Each learner has a four-digit parent passcode, generated for new learners and reset by an administrator; the original passcode cannot be retrieved. Parents use the school's contact email, learner admission number, and passcode to view published reports with calculated results. The separate annual ranking-sheet workflow, promotion rules, historical comparisons, and batch report downloads remain future work.

The supplied result sheet's term- and subject-specific grade/remark thresholds, competition ranking (including ties and no rank for non-positive results), and third-term subject averages rounded to one decimal place apply across classes. Use each learner's selected-subject count as the overall-average divisor. Test and examination score limits are 40 and 60.
