# Classfolio roadmap

1. **Product workspace and calculation rules — prototype milestone.** A branded overview, editable score sheet, student rankings, CSV export, printable report previews, and calculation helpers with workbook-rule regression tests. The fixture module supplies fictional records across three senior classes, three terms, and thirteen workbook subjects. Score changes remain in browser memory and reset on refresh.
2. **School records and computation — foundations in progress.** Netlify Database and Drizzle schema/migration are in place for classes, students, yearly enrollments, subjects, terms, staff assignments, draft scores, and audit events. A server-validated draft-score operation enforces the confirmed 40/60 limits and staff subject/class assignments. The visible workspace still reads fixtures; roster administration, workbook import, and wiring the score sheet to saved records remain to be built. Confirm score weighting, blank-score behavior, annual aggregation, and subject registration rules before implementing production calculations.
3. **Staff access and administration — foundations in progress.** Use Netlify Identity with invite-only accounts and `admin`/`teacher` roles. A server-side staff guard is in place for protected data operations. Add the sign-in/session UX, school setup, class/subject assignments, administrator workflows, and permissions enforced for every server operation.
4. **Results review and publishing.** Add completion tracking, result approval, locked published terms, comments, attendance, and printable school-branded reports. Keep drafts separate from published reports and audit score changes.
5. **Annual results and family access.** Implement the ranking-sheet workflow, agreed annual aggregation and promotion rules, historical comparisons, secure parent access, and batch report downloads. Validate all calculations against the original workbook and school-approved edge cases before production use.
