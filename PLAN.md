# Classfolio roadmap

1. **Product workspace and calculation rules — prototype milestone complete.** A branded overview, editable score sheet, student rankings, CSV export, and printable report previews demonstrate the intended workbook workflow. Demo records are fictional and score edits remain in memory.
2. **School registration and tenant foundations — implemented; live setup requires Netlify configuration.** Schools can register after email verification and receive isolated workspaces. Each workspace is seeded with JSS 1–3 and SS 1–3; JSS 1/2 use the supplied 17-subject template, JSS 3 remains open for its future subject list, and senior classes use the 13-subject workbook list. Administrators can configure subjects, enroll students, manage staff roles, and assign teachers. Netlify Database stores school records; Netlify Identity authenticates staff.
3. **Persistent score-entry foundation — implemented; integration incomplete.** Server operations save and validate unpublished draft scores with tenant, membership, role, assignment, enrollment, subject, and term checks. The visible overview and report preview remain fixture-backed; saved records are not yet used for report generation.
4. **School-branded reporting and results review.** Connect persisted roster and scores to the uploaded report layout, display the registered school's branding, add any additional report fields, and implement approval, publication, and audit workflows. Confirm junior grade, average, remark, and ranking rules with the school before calculating them.
5. **Annual results and family access.** Implement the ranking-sheet workflow, school-approved annual aggregation and promotion rules, historical comparisons, secure parent access, and batch report downloads. Validate formulas against the workbook and approved edge cases before production use.

The supplied senior workbook's term- and subject-specific grade/remark thresholds, competition ranking (including ties and no rank for non-positive results), third-term three-term subject averages rounded to one decimal place, and thirteen-subject aggregate divided by ten are intentional. Preserve their regression tests and do not normalize the thresholds or divisor. Test and examination score limits are 40 and 60.
